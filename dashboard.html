    Account & risk

    How the bot decides (fixed rules, no prediction)

    1 · Trend

    EMA 50 above EMA 200 with price above EMA 200 is an uptrend: buys only. The mirror image is a downtrend: sells only. Otherwise the bot stays out.

    2 · Pullback

    The bot waits for a dip against the trend: RSI below 40 in an uptrend, or above 60 in a downtrend.

    3 · Trigger

    When a candle closes with RSI back through the level, the signal fires. A candle that hasn't closed yet only shows a preview.

    4 · Exits

    Stop loss = 1.5 × ATR away. Target = 3 × the stop distance. Both are sent with the order, so Delta closes the trade even if the bot is off.

    It wins only about 30% of trades and earns through 3× winners. Long losing streaks are normal. Not financial advice.
