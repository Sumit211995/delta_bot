How does the bot decide? (no prediction, just fixed rules)
  1. Trend: when EMA 50 is above EMA 200 and price is above EMA 200, it's an uptrend and the bot only buys. The opposite is a downtrend, where it only sells. Anything else is "mixed", and the bot stays out.
  2. Pullback: in an uptrend, the bot waits for a dip (RSI drops below 40). When a candle closes with RSI back above 40, that's a BUY. A downtrend uses the mirror rule at 60 for a SELL.
  3. Stop loss = entry ∓ 1.5 × ATR (the coin's normal move size). Target = 3 × the stop distance.
  4. Size is chosen so that hitting the stop loses only the configured % of capital.
  5. The order is sent with the SL and TP attached. Delta closes the trade at one of them, even if the bot is off.
It wins only about 30% of trades but earns about 3× on winners. Long losing streaks are normal. Not financial advice.
